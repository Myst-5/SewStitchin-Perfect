About Sew Stitchin' Perfect

Welcome!

Welcome to Sew Stitchin' Perfect. My name is Zoeanne. I am a passionate Long-arm Quilter here for your everyday quilting needs. I have been quilting for nearly two decades. I have found so much joy in giving homemade quilts to my loved ones and would love to help you with your quilts, too. Feel free to contact me with questions. Let's chat!

Quilting Services

We offer custom edge-to-edge longarm quilting, high-quality batting selection options, precision trimming, and finished binding services designed to make your quilt cozy, durable, and beautiful.

Have questions about quilting or your project? Feel free to reach out to discuss your needs and thread color selections.
