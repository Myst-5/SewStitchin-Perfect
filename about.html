About Sew Stitchin' Perfect

Meet Zoeanne

Welcome to Sew Stitchin' Perfect! With a lifelong passion for textiles and precision craftsmanship, Zoeanne brings care, creativity, and professional artistry to every quilt that comes through the studio.

Every project is treated as a unique work of art, ensuring your cherished quilt tops are finished with pristine longarm quilting and expert binding.

Quilting Services

We offer custom edge-to-edge longarm quilting, high-quality batting selection options, precision trimming, and hand-finished binding services designed to make your quilt cozy, durable, and beautiful.

Have questions about quilting or your project? Feel free to reach out to discuss your needs and thread color selections.
